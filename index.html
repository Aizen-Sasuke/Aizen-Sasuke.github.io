01 — About

Code first,
questions later.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at International University of Scholars, Dhaka. I care about writing clean, functional software and understanding systems deeply before touching them — most of what's on this page is live, so you can poke at it instead of taking my word for it.

Lately that's meant a lot of canvas, WebGL and Web Audio work: physics simulations, generative visuals, small games. I also build Android apps, and I built the site for Aesthe Steps, a footwear brand, on Next.js with Payload CMS behind it.

When I'm not coding, I'm either reading Dostoevsky, stuck on a chess puzzle, or overthinking something I should've let go of yesterday.
